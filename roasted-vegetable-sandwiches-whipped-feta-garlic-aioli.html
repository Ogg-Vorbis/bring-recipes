Roasted Vegetable Sandwiches with Whipped Feta & Garlic Aioli

2 large sandwiches · 40 minutes · Easy

Toasted ciabatta with smoky roasted vegetables, tangy whipped feta, and garlic aioli. Make two vegetable sandwiches and add chicken or steak to just one, if you like.

Ingredients

Roasted vegetables

  • 1 medium zucchini, sliced lengthwise into ¼-inch strips
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced into wide strips
  • ½ medium red onion, cut into thick wedges
  • 4 oz cremini mushrooms, thickly sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Whipped feta

  • 3 oz feta cheese
  • 2 tbsp plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • Black pepper, to taste

Garlic aioli

  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 small garlic clove, finely grated
  • ½ tsp lemon juice

Assembly

  • 2 ciabatta rolls
  • 1 handful arugula or baby spinach
  • 1–2 tsp balsamic glaze (optional)
  • 4–5 oz cooked chicken breast or steak, thinly sliced (optional, for one sandwich)

Directions

  1. Roast the vegetables. Heat the oven to 425°F. Toss the zucchini, bell pepper, onion, and mushrooms with the olive oil, Italian seasoning, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper. Spread them in a single layer on a large sheet pan, using two pans if needed. Roast for 25–30 minutes, flipping halfway, until tender with browned edges.
  2. Make both spreads. While the vegetables roast, mash or blend the feta, yogurt, 1 tsp lemon juice, 1 tsp olive oil, and black pepper until creamy. Add 1 tsp water if needed to loosen it. In a separate bowl, stir the mayonnaise, grated garlic, and remaining ½ tsp lemon juice together.
  3. Toast and build both sandwiches. Split and toast the ciabatta rolls until lightly golden. Spread whipped feta on the bottom halves and garlic aioli on the tops. Divide the roasted vegetables and greens between the rolls and drizzle with balsamic glaze, if using.
  4. Add meat to one sandwich, if desired. Layer the warm, thinly sliced cooked chicken or steak over the vegetables on just one roll. Close both sandwiches and press gently.
Options and tips
  • Lemon-garlic chicken is the first choice. Flank steak or sirloin pairs well with the mushrooms and balsamic. For a no-cook meat option, use 2–3 slices of prosciutto.
  • For a heartier meatless sandwich, add fresh mozzarella or mashed white beans. Choose feta and mozzarella made with vegetarian rennet if needed.
  • Let the mushrooms roast until their released moisture evaporates. For a fall variation, swap the zucchini for thinly sliced delicata squash.
