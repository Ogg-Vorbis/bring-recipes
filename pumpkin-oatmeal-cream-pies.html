Pumpkin Oatmeal Cream Pies

Chill: 30–60 minutes · Oven: 350°F (177°C) · Bake: 12–15 minutes

Yield: Not specified · Original author: Not specified

Soft pumpkin oatmeal sandwich cookies with a spiced cream cheese filling.

Ingredients

Cookies

  • 1 cup pumpkin puree (blotted to about ¾ cup)
  • 2¼ cups old-fashioned rolled oats
  • 1⅔ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 2 tsp pumpkin pie spice
  • 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • ½ cup brown sugar
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Cream filling

  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • ¼ cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 1½ cups powdered sugar
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract
  • ½ tsp pumpkin pie spice
  • Pinch of salt

Directions

  1. Blot the pumpkin puree with paper towels until reduced from 1 cup to about ¾ cup.
  2. Whisk together the oats, flour, baking soda, salt, cinnamon, and pumpkin pie spice.
  3. Beat the butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar until creamy. Mix in the egg yolk, vanilla extract, and blotted pumpkin puree.
  4. Gradually add the dry ingredients and mix until combined. Chill the dough for 30–60 minutes.
  5. Preheat the oven to 350°F (177°C). Line baking sheets. Scoop the dough onto the prepared sheets and slightly flatten each portion.
  6. Bake for 12–15 minutes, until the edges are set. Cool completely before filling.
  7. For the filling, beat the cream cheese and butter until smooth. Add the powdered sugar, vanilla extract, pumpkin pie spice, and pinch of salt, and beat until smooth.
  8. Spread or pipe the filling onto half of the cooled cookies. Sandwich with the remaining cookies.

Notes

The supplied recipe does not specify the yield or scoop size. The baking time does not include chilling or cooling.
