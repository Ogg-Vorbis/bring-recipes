Pumpkin Pudding Pie with Orange Zest & Cardamom Whipped Cream

Yield: About 3 pies · Oven: 325°F · Bake: 55 minutes

Original author: Not specified · Crust size: not specified

A pumpkin pudding pie finished with powdered sugar and orange zest, served with cardamom whipped cream.

Ingredients

Pie and topping

  • About 3 prepared pie crusts (homemade pastry, premade dough, or graham cracker crust; size not specified)
  • 2½ cups sugar
  • 1 (15 oz) can pumpkin puree
  • 2 eggs
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • 2 cups flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp ground cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp vanilla
  • Pinch of salt
  • 2½ cups milk
  • 4 tbsp butter, melted
  • 1 orange, for zest
  • 1 tsp powdered sugar

Cardamom whipped cream

  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 2 tbsp powdered sugar
  • 1 tsp cardamom
  • Pinch of nutmeg

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 325°F. Prepare the pie crusts using your chosen crust recipe or package directions.
  2. In a large bowl, blend the sugar, pumpkin puree, eggs, and baking soda. If using a mixer, use the paddle attachment.
  3. Add the flour, baking powder, cinnamon, vanilla, and salt. Blend until well combined.
  4. Add the milk and melted butter and whisk until well mixed. If using a mixer, switch to the whisk attachment.
  5. Divide the liquid batter among the prepared crusts. Practical caution added to the source: leave headspace for expansion rather than filling to the rim; the batter rises as it bakes.
  6. Bake at 325°F for 55 minutes, as directed in the supplied recipe. Remove from the oven and let cool.
  7. Sift the 1 tsp powdered sugar over the cooled pies and zest the orange over the tops. A microplane or regular zester works; finely diced orange peel is an alternative mentioned in the source.
  8. For the whipped cream, chill a metal mixing bowl and whisk in the freezer for at least 15 minutes.
  9. Whisk the 2 tbsp powdered sugar, cardamom, and nutmeg together in the chilled bowl. Add the heavy cream and whisk until stiff peaks begin to form.
  10. Serve the cardamom whipped cream on the cooled pie.
