Greek Chicken Gyros with Homemade Tzatziki

Yield: 4 gyros · Source: User-provided recipe

Marinate: at least 30 minutes, ideally 2–4 hours (no longer than 8 hours) · Chicken: approximately 5–7 minutes per side · Rest: 5 minutes

Juicy, lemony chicken thighs marinated with Greek yogurt, garlic, oregano, and warm spices, then seared until golden with lightly charred edges. Served in warm pita with homemade tzatziki, fresh tomato, cucumber, and red onion.

Ingredients

Greek Chicken

  • 1½ lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp smoked or sweet paprika
  • ½ tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp kosher salt (use ¾ tsp if fine table salt)
  • ½ tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 tbsp olive oil, for cooking

Homemade Tzatziki

  • 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • ½ medium cucumber, grated (about ½ cup)
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 1 clove garlic, finely grated
  • 1 tbsp fresh dill, chopped (or 1 tsp dried)
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • ¼ tsp salt, plus more to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste

For Serving

  • 4 Greek-style pita breads
  • 2 medium tomatoes, diced or sliced
  • ½ small red onion, thinly sliced
  • ½ cucumber, thinly sliced
  • Shredded romaine lettuce (optional)
  • Lemon wedges
  • Crumbled feta (optional)

Directions

  1. Marinate the Chicken In a large bowl, combine Greek yogurt, olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, paprika, cumin, salt, and pepper. Add chicken thighs and toss until thoroughly coated. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes, ideally 2–4 hours. Avoid marinating longer than 8 hours because the lemon juice can affect the texture.
  2. Prepare the Tzatziki Grate the cucumber using the large holes of a box grater. Place grated cucumber in a clean kitchen towel or paper towels and squeeze firmly to remove excess moisture. Combine cucumber, Greek yogurt, lemon juice, garlic, dill, olive oil, salt, and pepper. Refrigerate for at least 15–20 minutes while preparing the remaining ingredients.
  3. Cook the Chicken Remove chicken from the refrigerator about 15 minutes before cooking. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large cast-iron skillet or heavy-bottomed pan over medium-high heat. Remove chicken from marinade, allowing excess marinade to drip off. Leave a thin coating on the meat. Place chicken thighs in the hot skillet in a single layer, working in batches if necessary. Cook approximately 5–7 minutes per side, adjusting heat as needed, until browned with lightly charred edges. Chicken must reach at least 165°F internally. Thighs are often more tender around 175–185°F. Transfer to a cutting board and rest for 5 minutes. Slice into thin strips. For extra crispy edges, return the strips to the hot skillet for 1–2 minutes.
  4. Warm the Pita Heat a dry skillet over medium heat. Warm each pita for approximately 20–30 seconds per side until soft and pliable. Keep wrapped in a clean towel until ready to assemble.
  5. Assemble the Gyros Spread a generous spoonful of tzatziki down the center of each warm pita. Add sliced chicken, tomato, cucumber, and red onion. Add lettuce or feta if desired. Finish with an additional drizzle of tzatziki and a squeeze of fresh lemon. Fold into a wrap and serve immediately.

Tips & Variations

  • For more authentic gyro-style texture: Slice the cooked chicken thinly and quickly sear the strips again to create crispy, browned edges.
  • For softer red onions: Soak sliced onions in cold water for 10 minutes, then drain.
  • For extra flavor: Add a little lemon zest to the chicken marinade.
  • For a traditional Greek street-food touch: Add a handful of crispy French fries inside each gyro.
  • For meal prep: Store chicken, tzatziki, vegetables, and pita separately. Refrigerate cooked chicken for up to 3–4 days.

Best results: Marinate the thighs for 2–4 hours, cook them whole to retain their juices, then slice and briefly crisp the edges before assembling.
