Sticky Pineapple Teriyaki Drumsticks with Roasted Carrots & Broccoli

2 generous servings · Easy · Prep: about 10 minutes · Roasting: about 40–50 minutes · Rest: 5 minutes · Total: about 55–65 minutes · Oven: 425°F (220°C)

Pineapple-teriyaki seasoned drumsticks finished with a sticky pineapple glaze, served with roasted carrots, broccoli, and jasmine rice.

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 5–6 chicken drumsticks (about 1½–2 lb)
  • 2 tbsp McCormick Grill Mates Pineapple Teriyaki seasoning
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil

Pineapple teriyaki glaze

  • ⅓ cup pineapple juice
  • 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp honey or brown sugar (optional)
  • 1 tsp cornstarch
  • 1 tbsp cold water (for cornstarch slurry)
  • ½ tsp freshly grated ginger (optional)

Roasted vegetables

  • 3 medium carrots, cut into diagonal ½-inch pieces
  • 1 large head broccoli (about 12 oz), cut into florets
  • 1½ tbsp olive or neutral oil, divided
  • Black pepper, to taste

To serve

  • ¾ cup uncooked jasmine rice
  • Water for rice, according to package instructions
  • Sesame seeds, for garnish
  • 2 green onions, sliced
  • Lime wedges (optional)

Directions

  1. Prepare and season. Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C), with racks arranged for two baking sheets. Pat the drumsticks dry. Rub with 1 tbsp neutral oil and the Pineapple Teriyaki seasoning. Arrange on a foil-lined, lightly oiled rimmed baking sheet with space between the pieces. Do not add extra salt to the chicken.
  2. Begin roasting and start rice. Put the chicken in the oven and start a timer; all times below are measured from this point. Expect about 40–50 minutes of roasting, depending on size and oven. Meanwhile, cook the jasmine rice with water according to its package instructions. Prepare the vegetables while the chicken roasts.
  3. Make and divide the glaze. Combine pineapple juice, soy sauce, rice vinegar, optional honey or brown sugar, and optional ginger in a small saucepan. Bring to a gentle simmer. Mix cornstarch with 1 tbsp cold water, stir into the sauce, and simmer for 30–60 seconds until lightly thickened and brushable. If it becomes too thick, stir in a splash of water. Before brushing any chicken, divide the glaze between two bowls: one for brushing and one kept clean for serving.
  4. At 20 minutes: add carrots. Toss the carrots with 1 tbsp of the vegetable oil and black pepper. Spread on a second large baking sheet, leaving room for the broccoli, and place in the oven. Keep the vegetables on their own pan, away from raw chicken juices.
  5. At 30 minutes: add broccoli. Toss broccoli with the remaining ½ tbsp vegetable oil and black pepper, then add it to the carrot pan in a single layer. Turn the carrots and rotate the pans front to back if needed for even browning. Roast the vegetables for about 15–20 minutes more, until the carrots are tender and the broccoli has browned edges. Remove either vegetable early if it is done.
  6. At about 35 minutes: glaze chicken. Brush the drumsticks lightly with glaze from the brushing bowl. Roast for 5–7 minutes, brush again, and roast about 5 minutes more. Watch closely: the seasoning and glaze both contain sugar. If the coating darkens too quickly, loosely cover the chicken with foil and finish cooking without adding more glaze.
  7. Check doneness and rest. Check the thickest part of several drumsticks with a thermometer, avoiding the bone. They must reach at least 165°F; about 175–185°F generally gives drumsticks a more tender texture. Remove cooked pieces individually if needed, and give larger pieces additional time. Rest the chicken for 5 minutes. If vegetables still need time, leave them in while the chicken rests.
  8. Serve. Serve the chicken with jasmine rice, roasted carrots, and broccoli. Spoon over the clean reserved glaze and garnish with sesame seeds and sliced green onions. Add optional lime wedges. Discard any leftover glaze from the brushing bowl.

Cooking notes

  • The roasting stages overlap, rather than adding up. After the chicken goes in: carrots at minute 20, broccoli at minute 30, glaze at about minute 35, then check everything around minute 45.
  • Use two roomy baking sheets so the chicken and vegetables roast rather than steam. Timing is approximate; use tenderness and internal temperature to decide when each component is ready.
  • The supplied seasoning amount is within McCormick's recommendation of 1–2 tbsp per pound. It already contains salt and sugar. Omit the optional honey for a less-sweet glaze.
  • This makes roughly ½ cup of glaze before reduction, divided between brushing and serving: a light drizzle for the rice, not a large pool of sauce. For extra sauce, double all glaze ingredients, including the slurry.
  • Optional variation: replace broccoli with halved Brussels sprouts and add them with the carrots, checking after 25–30 minutes. For extra pineapple flavor, add ½ cup pineapple chunks to the vegetable pan for its final 10–12 minutes.
  • This is an adaptation of the supplied recipe, not a published McCormick recipe or a personally kitchen-tested recipe.
