Green Chicken Enchilada Casserole

Pan: 9 × 13 inches · Oven: 350°F · Bake: about 40 minutes, covered

Servings: Not specified · Original author: Not specified

A layered chicken enchilada casserole with corn tortillas, Monterey Jack cheese, and Las Palmas green enchilada sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken
  • Corn tortillas, about 6 per layer (approximately 18 for 3 layers)
  • 1–2 lb Monterey Jack cheese, shredded, depending on how cheesy you want it
  • Las Palmas green enchilada sauce, 1 large can and 1 small can
  • Salt, for cooking the chicken
  • Garlic powder, for cooking the chicken
  • Nonstick cooking spray
  • Optional: sliced onion, for cooking the chicken
  • Optional: sliced olives, 2 small cans

Directions

  1. Boil the whole chicken in water seasoned with salt and garlic powder. Add sliced onion if desired. Cook until the chicken is tender enough for the meat to come off the bone easily.
  2. When cool enough to handle, remove the meat from the bones. Cut it into bite-size pieces or shred it with forks.
  3. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Spray the bottom of a 9 × 13-inch baking dish with nonstick cooking spray, then add just enough enchilada sauce to cover the bottom.
  4. Lay about 6 corn tortillas over the sauce. Add a layer of chicken, then shredded cheese and optional sliced olives. Cover with some enchilada sauce, then another layer of about 6 tortillas.
  5. Repeat with another layer of chicken, cheese, optional olives, and enchilada sauce. Cover with a third layer of about 6 tortillas.
  6. Top the final tortilla layer with shredded cheese, then more enchilada sauce.
  7. Cover the dish with foil and bake at 350°F for about 40 minutes.

Notes

  • The original recipe does not specify the chicken size, exact can sizes, seasoning amounts, serving count, or chicken cooking time.
  • Approximately 18 tortillas is an estimate based on 3 layers of about 6 tortillas.
  • The 40-minute timing is for baking the assembled casserole; cooking and shredding the chicken takes additional time.

Added food-safety note: Use a food thermometer to check that both the chicken and the center of the finished casserole reach at least 165°F. USDA safe minimum temperature chart.
