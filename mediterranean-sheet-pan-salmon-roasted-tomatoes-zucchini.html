Mediterranean Sheet-Pan Salmon with Roasted Tomatoes & Zucchini

Servings: 2 · Prep: 10 minutes · Cook: 25–30 minutes · Total: 35–40 minutes · Oven: 425°F (220°C)

Salmon with roasted zucchini, cherry tomatoes, red onion, Kalamata olives, lemon, and herbs, with optional feta and lemon couscous.

Ingredients

Salmon & vegetables

  • 2 salmon fillets (5–6 oz each)
  • 1 medium zucchini, cut into ½-inch half-moons
  • 1½ cups cherry tomatoes, left whole
  • ½ medium red onion, cut into thin wedges
  • ¼ cup pitted Kalamata olives, halved
  • 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, divided
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • ¼ tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp kosher salt, divided, plus more to taste
  • ¼ tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
  • 2 tbsp crumbled feta cheese (optional)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C). Line a large rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper or lightly grease it.
  2. Prepare vegetables. In a large bowl, combine zucchini, cherry tomatoes, and red onion. Toss with 1½ tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp dried oregano, ¼ tsp smoked paprika, ¼ tsp kosher salt, and ⅛ tsp black pepper. Spread the vegetables in a single layer on the baking sheet, leaving room for the salmon.
  3. Start roasting. Roast vegetables for 12–15 minutes, until the zucchini begins to soften and the tomatoes start to blister.
  4. Prepare salmon. While the vegetables roast, pat salmon fillets dry with paper towels. Rub with the remaining ½ tbsp olive oil, remaining ¼ tsp kosher salt, remaining ⅛ tsp black pepper, and 1 tsp fresh lemon juice.
  5. Add salmon, garlic & olives. Remove the baking sheet from the oven. Toss the minced garlic into the hot vegetables, distributing it among them. Scatter the Kalamata olives around the pan. Place salmon fillets skin-side down in the cleared spaces between the vegetables.
  6. Finish roasting. Return the baking sheet to the oven for approximately 10–14 minutes, depending on the thickness of the salmon. Start checking at 10 minutes. For fully cooked salmon, the thickest part should reach 145°F. If the vegetables need more time but the salmon is done, remove the salmon and return the vegetables to the oven for a few minutes.
  7. Finish and serve. Remove from the oven and squeeze 1–2 tbsp fresh lemon juice over the salmon and vegetables. Sprinkle with chopped parsley and optional crumbled feta. Spoon the roasted tomato juices over the salmon before serving.

Optional Side: Lemon Couscous

For a slightly more substantial dinner:

  • ½ cup dry couscous (optional lemon couscous side)
  • ½ cup water or chicken broth (optional lemon couscous side)
  • 1 tsp olive oil (optional lemon couscous side)
  • 1 tsp lemon juice (optional lemon couscous side)
  • Pinch of salt (optional lemon couscous side)
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley (optional) (optional lemon couscous side)
  1. Bring water or broth, olive oil, and salt to a boil.
  2. Remove from heat and stir in couscous.
  3. Cover and let sit for 5 minutes.
  4. Fluff with a fork, then stir in lemon juice and parsley. Serve the salmon and roasted vegetables over or alongside the couscous.

Cooking Notes

  • Use a large baking sheet (approximately 13 × 18 inches) to avoid overcrowding.
  • Leave cherry tomatoes whole so they soften and burst during roasting.
  • Add minced garlic after the initial vegetable roast to prevent burning.
  • Salmon cooking time depends on thickness; a thermometer is more reliable than timing alone.
  • The feta and olives provide saltiness, so taste before adding extra salt.
  • The roasted tomato juices, lemon, and olive oil naturally form a light sauce without needing butter or cream.
