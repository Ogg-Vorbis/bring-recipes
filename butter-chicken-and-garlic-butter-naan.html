Butter Chicken and Garlic Butter Naan

A budget-friendly butter chicken meal costing approximately £2.80 per portion.

Yield: 5 servingsSource: Michelin on a Minimum Wage
Bring! Shopping List App for iPhone and Android

Chicken marinade

  • 2 tbsp Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp garam masala
  • 1 tbsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tbsp ginger and garlic paste

Sauce

  • 2 red onions, finely diced
  • 1 can chopped tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp garam masala
  • 1 tbsp turmeric
  • 3/4 tbsp chilli powder
  • 3/4 tbsp salt
  • 1 tsp fenugreek
  • 1 tsp brown sugar
  • 150 ml double cream (heavy whipping cream)
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 10 chicken thighs, chopped
  • Chopped coriander, for garnish

Garlic butter naan

  • Naan bread
  • 2 tbsp butter, melted
  • 3 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp fenugreek

Source note: The supplied recipe lists the garlic butter naan ingredients but does not include directions for preparing or heating the naan.

Method

  1. Coat the chopped chicken thighs in the Greek yogurt, garam masala, turmeric, salt, chilli powder, and ginger and garlic paste. Marinate for at least 30 minutes, preferably overnight.
  2. Arrange the marinated chicken on a baking tray and grill at 210°C for 25 minutes.
  3. Fry the diced onions until golden brown. Add the chopped tomatoes, then fill three-quarters of the empty tomato can with water and add it to the pan. Mix well, stir in the sauce spices and brown sugar, and cook over low heat for 10 minutes.
  4. Allow the sauce to cool, then blend until smooth.
  5. Return the sauce to the pan and add the cooked chicken. Mix well, then stir in the double cream and butter. Finish with fresh coriander and cook over low heat for another 10 minutes.
  6. Serve and enjoy.
